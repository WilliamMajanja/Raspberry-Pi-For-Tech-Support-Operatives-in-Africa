- The root `LICENSE` is a scope notice, not a blanket license. Original material written for this edition is CC0; individual third-party terms remain in force.
- New pages link to upstream sources and summarize them in original language instead of reproducing their text.
- Third-party repositories, course material, images, code, and books retain their own licenses and attribution requirements.
- International Mine Action Standards are UN-owned and copyrighted; the cited IMAS pages state their reuse conditions. POTI training materials may be provided under revocable licenses. This guide links to those sources and summarizes themes in original language; do not copy their course pages, PDFs, scenarios, figures, or standards text into the repository.
- Fork status is identified where known. A public repository is not automatically open-source, and a fork is not sole authorship.
- Before adding outside text, assets, datasets, or code, verify their source, license, and required attribution with the rights holder or upstream project.
