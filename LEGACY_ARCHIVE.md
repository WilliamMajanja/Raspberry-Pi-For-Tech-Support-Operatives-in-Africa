# Legacy Kali Tool Material

The original book was a Kali-tool catalogue assembled around the Kali 1.x/2.x period. Its surviving pages are preserved as historical source material; they have not been reviewed for current package availability, command accuracy, safety, or third-party license compatibility. They are **not part of the maintained Raspberry Pi support curriculum**.

During this refresh, **276 empty Markdown placeholders were removed** because they contained no article text and falsely suggested complete coverage. The old chapter folders remain as historical indexes. In the current tree, substantive legacy article files remain only in the exploitation-tools folder, one information-gathering entry, and the Awesome Lists page. Some of these old articles discuss exploitation, persistence, social engineering, or other dual-use techniques. Do not treat the presence of a page as a recommendation or run its commands on a system.
