# William Majanja — Informatics Portfolio

**Raspberry Pi for Technical Support Operatives in Africa** is written and curated by William Majanja as an initiative of the **Hack with Raspberry Pi** community. William’s public work across [@WilliamMajanja](https://github.com/WilliamMajanja) and [@WilliamMajanja-zz](https://github.com/WilliamMajanja-zz) explores how information and software can support practical work in health, civic life, land, agriculture, publishing, education, and computing infrastructure.

The projects below are selected examples, not a claim that every repository on either account is original, complete, deployed, or independently validated. Forks, collaborations, research prototypes, and proprietary projects are identified. Descriptions summarize what the repositories say about themselves; they do not certify results or suitability for real-world decisions.
